# HALCYON · Kraken Port

Day 2 of a 7-day build challenge. A fictional shuttle line's home page that is also the place it flies to: Kraken Port,
a Japanese cyberpunk city floating on Titan's methane sea, built live in the browser with Three.js (WebGPU, TSL shaders).
The design language was extracted from the Rive City site with `skillui`; the product, world and art are new.

**Open it, then:** press **Board HL-07** (or Enter) to dive through the great torii and fly the canals.

- **Drive:** W A S D (touch: the stick), **F** skim above the water, **P** autopilot, **C** camera, **N** day / night,
  drag to look, click a screen to open it, **U** cycles the three HUD styles.
- **Chapters** in the top bar fly you to Kraken Vision, the Crab, the Tenryū pagoda (its dragon flies round it) and
  Mugen Ward, where the city carries on without end. An arrow over the ticker and a marker in the world show the way.
- **Sound:** the button by the clock plays *I Really Want to Stay at Your House* (Rosa Walton) through YouTube's own
  player. Your own copy can go in `assets/audio/` instead (see the README there).

Built with Three.js r170 (WebGPU), Blender 4.4 (the dragon and the crab, scripts in `tools/`), and AI-generated key art
for the billboards. No build step: serve the folder.
